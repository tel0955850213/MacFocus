## Description

Focus Arcana turns a Pomodoro timer into a rewarding desktop focus ritual, completely free.

Choose a focus intention, start a session, and make progress that matters. Every completed session earns XP and coins, strengthens your daily streak, and expands a growing collection of heroes across three fantasy realms.

FEATURES

- Pomodoro focus, short breaks, and long breaks with a clear animated countdown.
- Daily goals, XP levels, streaks, and focus history that make progress visible.
- Work, study, and creative labels with a 12-week focus heatmap.
- A mouse-driven summoning gate: pull a sealed card downward to reveal a new hero.
- Collect goddesses, heroes, and smallfolk characters from Asterra, Elyrion, and Kharvane.
- Build a bond with your selected companion as you focus together.
- Keep a chibi desktop companion in the corner of your Mac with timer-aware dialogue.
- Menu bar timer, launch-at-login option, and local focus ambience, all included for free.
- Local-first progress: your sessions, settings, and collection stay on your Mac.

Focus Arcana is made for people who want a focus timer with more personality than a countdown alone.

## Age Rating Rationale

The app includes stylized fantasy character artwork featuring adults in fully clothed fantasy outfits. It has no nudity, explicit sexual content, real-money gambling, user-generated content, web browsing, messaging, or social features. The card collection uses only focus-earned in-app coins; users cannot purchase random draws.

## App Review Notes

No login is required. Focus Arcana works offline for its core timer, collection, companion, and statistics features.

To test the primary loop:

1. Open Focus and start a timer.
2. Open Summon and pull the central sealed card downward with the mouse to reveal a hero.
3. Open Collection to choose an unlocked hero as the companion.
4. Use Summon companion in the sidebar to show the desktop companion window.

Focus Arcana has no login, subscription, or in-app purchase. Every current feature is available for free.
