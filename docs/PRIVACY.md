# MacFocus Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 15, 2026_

MacFocus is a macOS productivity app that turns focus sessions into a lightweight character-collection experience.

## Data We Store

MacFocus currently stores the following data locally on your Mac:

- Focus session history, including dates and session lengths
- XP, coins, level, streaks, unlocked characters, and companion selection
- App settings such as language, timer durations, notification preferences, and daily goal
- Purchase entitlement state reported by Apple's StoreKit framework

## Data We Do Not Collect

MacFocus does not currently operate a server and does not collect, sell, or share personal data. Focus data stays on your device unless a future version adds optional cloud sync.

## Purchases

MacFocus Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card information. Apple may provide subscription status information needed to unlock Pro features.

## Notifications

If enabled, MacFocus may send local macOS notifications when a focus session completes. Notification permission is requested by macOS and can be changed in System Settings.

## Future Cloud Sync

Cloud sync is planned but not yet available. If added, this policy will be updated before any cloud account or synchronization feature is enabled.
