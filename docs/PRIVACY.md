# Focus Arcana Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 10, 2026_

Focus Arcana is a macOS productivity app that turns focus sessions into a lightweight character-collection experience.

## Data We Store

Focus Arcana stores the following data locally on your Mac:

- Focus session history, including dates and session lengths
- XP, coins, level, streaks, unlocked characters, and companion selection
- App settings such as language, timer durations, notification preferences, labels, ambience preferences, and daily goal

## Data We Do Not Collect

Focus Arcana does not operate a server and does not collect, sell, or share personal data. Focus data stays on your device.

## Notifications

If enabled, MacFocus may send local macOS notifications when a focus session completes. Notification permission is requested by macOS and can be changed in System Settings.
